One of my earliest stitches of 2025. The color combo of this is perfect. I used all the called for fabric and floss. Patterns with embroidery designs are some of my favorites. The
pin cushion nest sold me on this one. There is a sort of companion by the same designer called Gathering Stitches that has a similar design but is much smaller. I will probably stitch
that one some day.
