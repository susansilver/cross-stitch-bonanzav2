This project was started October 17<sup>th</sup>, 2025. The spooky scene was perfect for a Halloween themed stitch. This piece is romantic to me and that is the direction
I went with when choosing fabric. I ended up going with a 40 count Chablis Linen by Picture This Plus. The delicate one over two stitching adds to the romance. I'm in love with
this one and cannot wait to finish and put this on the wall.
