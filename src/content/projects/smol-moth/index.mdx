This pattern is from the BAD Stitch Patreon and is no longer available. I knew that this would make a great needle minder.
I was inspired by a Flosstuber who makes her own magnets. I chose perforated paper to make it easier on putting it together.
This was a quick stitch and was completed in less than two weeks. I love this smol moth.

- Pattern: Smol Moth
- Designer: BAD Stitch
- Fabric: <a hef="https://123stitch.com/item/Mill-Hill-Perforated-paper-White/PP1">14ct Perforated Paper</a>
- Start Date: 10/01/25
- Finish Date: 10/15/25
