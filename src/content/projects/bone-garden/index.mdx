This was my New Year start. Y'know following the trend of starting something at the beginning of the year. I had this kitted
and it felt right. It is a smaller pattern, about 4,000 stitches. It was a Patreon pattern from Flossy Fox Shop. It is
now on sale in their shop. I love that you could also stich this for Halloween because of the skeletons. I almost gave up
on it because of how dark the fabric is. I need to work with a neck light to see the holes. I love this fabric.
