What sold me on this project are the blackbirds. I'm not in love with the whole pattern. Something that I did not know before I started stitching. But, I've eaten my vegetables
on this one and have completed everything but the birds. This is being worked on as part of a SAL with the Catkin and Lillie Patreon community. I expect to finish before the
New Year.
