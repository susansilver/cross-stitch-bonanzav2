I saw this pattern on Flosstube and fell immediately in love. Alice in Wonderland is my favorite children's book. I watched the Disney VHS tap soo much that I wore it out.
As a child, I felt like Alice in a world of adult nonsense. What makes this pattern so wonderful is that it is based on the book and not the animated film. There are scenes
that you will only find in the book. This is a must stitch for an Alice fan.

- Pattern: <a href="https://owlforest.co.uk/besplatnaya-skhema-dlya-vyshivaniya-alisa-v-strane-chudes/">Alice in Wonderland SAL</a>
