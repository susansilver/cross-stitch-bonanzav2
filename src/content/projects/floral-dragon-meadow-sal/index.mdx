This is a pattern that has gotten a lot of work. It started in August and goes until December.
It is a six month SAL by Flossy Fox Shop. As of writing this, I have not missed a month. Each
section is large at 3,000+ stitches. It is difficult to keep up. Going strong and hoping to see this
one through.

I like this pattern for its retro pixelated style and floral inspired dragons.
