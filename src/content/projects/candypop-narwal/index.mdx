This project was started May 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026. This was part of the whale sal that I stitched along with <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZGOkQ3BLwMSBkpC4BSyZXg">Stitching with Charlie</a>. She

on Patreon and could be released to the public in the next few months.
