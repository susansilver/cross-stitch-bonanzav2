I've wanted to do a Long Dog Sampler for a long time. It was difficult to choose one. Opening Gambit
appealed to me because it was chess themed. Which reminds me of Through the Looking Glass the sequel to Alice
in Wonderland. The narrative is also a chess game. Of course, chess is also a favorite subject in mathematics.

Tan did their Long Dog Sampler on a 25 count fabric. That inspired me to do the same
for this one. I like it better than a 40 count. I'm doing one over one on a white opalescent lugana.

- Pattern: <a href="https://www.longdogsampler.com/product/opening-gambit/">Opening Gambit</a>
- Designer: Long Dog Samplers
- Fabric: <a href="https://123stitch.com/item/Zweigart-25-Count-White-Opalescent-Lugana-Fabric-18x27/123-2139Z">18 x 27 25ct White Opalescent Lugana by Zweigart</a>
