I cross stich for stress relief most of the time. In March, I picked up the needle
again after a long hiatus. Things were getting to me and I started again. I was ambitious
this time and wanted to try working with Linen. I was afraid but it seemed worth trying.
It was not a problem. I've started only working with linen and lugana for my projects.
