The name of this pattern is Tea Reading Frog. It was a free download from [The Stitch Crypt Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/thestitchcrypt). It is no longer available and I'm not sure if it will go on sale.
Keep an eye out for it. I will say this designer's style is similar to The Witchy Stitcher. I like that gothic style a lot. This is a case where the fabric chose the pattern.
Fiberlicious released several fabrics with flecks of gold. I knew the dark blue would contrast with the white of the china. The gold flecks would bring out the golden edges
of the tea cup. It was such a good match.

- Designer: The Stitch Crypt
- Fabric: 32 Count Linen - The Seer's Vision (Gold Dusted) by Fiberlicious Yummy Fabrics
