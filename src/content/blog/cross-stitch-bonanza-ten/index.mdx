Well, what is there to say. Welcome to the new blog! Frankly, I've never liked using other people's designs. It isn't so
much about aasthetics for me. What matters more is function.

## Why I Designed This New Website

The previous design could not handle the things that I needed it to do. Building this website from scratch meant
that I could add more to the backend. There is stuff going on behind the scenes that make my life easier when updating this website.

The main focus of the design was on highlighting the WIPs. They are now the fist thing that you see when you visit the home page. I used photographs to entice people to make a
selection. This takes you to a project page with information and a recent image. The project pages are accessible from most pages now.

Lastly, I added a way to subscribe to a weekly newsletter. Every Sunday, you will get an update when the new blog is live. These emails are brief. They are going to be
about one paragraph of text and a link to the new post. That's it. No marketing or spam. Just an alert for your convenance.
