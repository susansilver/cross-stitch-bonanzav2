## plans

I'm in two cross stitch communities. One on Patreon and the other on Discord. I asked both,
how do you work on multiple WIPs? The most common answer was vibes. People stitch on what
has their interest. I'm not comfortable with that as I find that I need more structure.

So, I thought about my stitching capacity. I can stitch about 6,000 stitches a month. So, that
means that I can work on three projects for 2,000 stitches or six for 1,000 stitches. I need to do at
least 1,000 to feel like I made some progress.

My plan right now is to choose three projects a month to work
on two days a week. Reserving Sunday to work on my [Alice in Wonderland SAL](https://suzzastitches.com/projects/alice-in-wonderland-sal).
I might choose which WIPs to work on by spinning a wheel while reserving the right to choose one that I want
to work on instead. You know, ultimately, you have to stitch on what makes you happy. I like
having a plan to help me make decisions.

I am going to give up on finishing the part of the Floral Meadow Dragon SAL that I'm on. I gave
it two weeks and didn't finish this time. I think that is enough dedication. I don't know
if I will try to catch up. It depends on if I like the next part which is a Rose Dragon. I do love
the Flossy Fox Shop style which makes this an enjoyable stitch. My only problem is making the time
for it.
