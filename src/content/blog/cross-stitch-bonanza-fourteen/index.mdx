Lately, I've had a good mood. Feeling positive and optimistic. Although, thoughts of what is happening in my country and the
world is on my mind. But, I'm keeping the doom thoughts away and I'm focused on the things that I can do to make the
world better. I'm enjoying hanging out with my mom and working on my cross stitch projects. We are watching Hell's Kitchen
together. We recently finished season 23 and have moved on to the current season. We have quiet days around here and a
routine that keeps me happy.

In Febuary, I went to my first Fiber Arts meetup at a local craft store in my town. There were only about
six people. Who were all knitting. I'm socially awkward so I'm never sure how to talk in groups. I don't
usually start conversations for that reason. Luckily, these women were inclusive and asked me questions. They
were mostly impressed with my 40-count little birb. They asked me a lot of questions about it. One woman asked me
if I use a sharp needle to pierce the fabric. I explained that you use the holes like normal and a very tiny needle
threaded with one thread. It was nice to do a little education.

There is another meetup in March that I plan on going to. I want to show the group my finish. And a woman there
said she would bring me a beginner's embroidery kit. I'm looking forward to it.

## Finishes

I surprised myself and completed Blueberry Harvest in one month. Jed X Stitch patterns are a pleasure to work on.
The colors are so vibrant. 40-count is now my favorite fabric. It is so much
easier to use variageted thread with one strand. I do subscribe to a fabric of the month club and I'm going to
switch counts. That way I can build a nice stash to work from. Anway, this is my finish.

## WIPs

I went back to Bone Garden by Flossy Fox Shop. This is a pattern that I had put away because the fabric is so dark.
And I was going to replace it with a 14-count Aida. But I lost the Aida. I decided to try again and brought it
to my meetup. The thinking was they may have better light and it would be easier to see. Turns out, I was right.
The light was the issue. So, I bought a little neck light. This is working out very well and I've made a lot
of progress. I've completed a third of about 3,300 stitches. I'm going to finish the left side of the top skeleton
and then move on to a project for my friend.

## Haul

I don't have anything to show here but I did purchase the [Elven Rabbits](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4404831449/eleven-rabbits-counted-cross-stitch) pattern from Jed X Stitch. I'm not
sure if I will start it soon or not. I've bought all the materials that I need to stitch it though. It includes
a few Gentle Art threads. It will be my firat time working with them. I'm also working on the called for fabric.
I don't typically do that but this was an instance were I could not decide what would look better. So, I took
the easy route. Working it with all the called for. I am patiently waiting for all of it to arrive.
