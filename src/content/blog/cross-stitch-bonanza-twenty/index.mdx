Doing things a little different here. I have WIPs but they are after story time. I am giving a content warning due to recounting a story about the death of my cat.
Pet death is a heavy thing and I don't want to trigger anyone. It is difficult for me now to look at any pets cat or otherwise.
Still trying to cope with things and I thought it would help to talk about my Misty in the blog today. I hope you
will read to hear about how awesome she was.

I apologize for the time away from biweekly blogging. The truth is simple. I'm not stitching very much and had nothing
to show. I'm still debating how frequently to update things. Is it okay if I add only 200-300 stitches? What if I only work
on one project? A debate that will go on in my mind. It might be best for people to subscribe to get notificatins
when there is an update. I can no longer promise a length of time inbetween sharing.

### Bone garden

I talked about moving into the main trailer with my Mother. There is much more light there. That lead me to
taking this project out again. The fabric is beautiful and suits the mood of this piece. The fabric is difficult
to work with because it is so dark. But, very much worth it. I want to see if I can get it finished before the end
of the year. It is only about 3,000 stitches. The hardest part is doin the fiddly bits that are left. Lots of color changes.
