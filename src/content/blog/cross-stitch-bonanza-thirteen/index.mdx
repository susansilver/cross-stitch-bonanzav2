I am pretty happy with the choice to post every two weeks. It means
there are a few more projects to share with you. Though, I am much
more monogamous than last year. I have moved to the idea of goal
oriented stitching. I look at my patterns and chose a section to work on.
I move on when it is completed. It is helping me chose which projects to
work on. And I worry less about time and more about finishing what I
want to work on.

## Starts

The big news is that I have my first start of the New Year. Which
also happens to be my birthday start! I ended up going through all
my patterns digital and paper. And, I didn't find one that was calling to
me. I ended up going for a pattern that I saw in Just Cross Stitch Magazine - Summer 2025 edition.
I get access to these issues through my library. The pattern is called Blueberry Harvest
and it is by Jessica Dize (Jed X Stitch). I've gotten pretty far and may finish by
the end of the month.
