## Plans

The hope is now that I've settled that I will get into temperature stitching again. The plan is to stitch on Evergreen
in the mornings and temperature stitching in the afternoon. Maybe kind of lame but all that I can muster right now. I'm so
jealous of people who do 3,000+ stitches in a week. I don't know how they find the time. I can only to a 1-2 hour stitchy
session before I have to take a break. Sitting for so long in one position is hard on my body. I have to do stretches! I'd
like to see a month where I do more than 5,0000 stitches.
